### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment is professional and friendly. FCJ members are always willing to help when we face difficulties — even after working hours. The office creates a serious, focused atmosphere that helps us study and work effectively.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
Mentors provide detailed guidance and share practical experience. Team admin supports logistics and creates favorable conditions for effective work.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
Assigned tasks match university knowledge while expanding into new domains. This helped me practice both professional and practical skills.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I practiced new skills such as project tools (Notion, draw.io, GitHub), teamwork, and professional communication in a corporate environment.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
Positive and open culture. People help each other, work joyfully yet effectively. The three‑month internship brought valuable experiences.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
Company events are very useful. Joining events helps not only with domain knowledge but also with understanding how a professional, large‑scale event operates — and experiencing memorable moments.

### Additional Questions
- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?
	- I was most satisfied that mentors always provided dedicated guidance to interns.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?
	- The company should have a dedicated announcement channel for important information so interns can keep up more effectively.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why?
	- Yes. Interning at AWS is a valuable opportunity to work and learn at a large, professional company. I have already recommended a friend for the next internship term and they will apply to AWS.

### Suggestions & Expectations
I don’t have any suggestions to improve the internship experience. If possible, I would like to join this program again in the future.
